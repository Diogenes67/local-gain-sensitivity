**Supplemental note 5: Experimental record and provenance**

This note is the experimental record. It states which estimator each experiment was run under and when, which earlier runs were superseded and by what, what survives of each superseded run, and where every number in the paper can be regenerated from. The Experimental procedures and the supplemental methods describe only the protocols in force; the history is here.

**Estimator versions and the runs that used them.** Three implementations of the gain estimator were used over the life of the project, and every result in the paper is labelled by the one that produced it (Table S7). (1) *March 2026, whole-input iteration on J.* Forward-mode power iteration on J over the whole layer input, on uniformly random token ids, without the attention mask. It converges to the spectral radius of the whole-sequence layer Jacobian, which is a median 2.1–3.6 times smaller than σ~1~ of the token-local block Jacobian and up to 18 times smaller at a block (Figure S2a). The routing-topology, skip-coefficient and destroyed-data experiments were first run under it in March 2026 and the k-gram sweep in May 2026; all four were re-measured or re-trained in September 2026 and are reported from the re-runs only. (2) *Single-position token-local iteration inside a training script.* A token-local J^T^J power iteration at the single position T/2, run by the script that trained the model. Three results use it, each marked in Table S7: the March mask × loss factorial, on random token ids without the attention mask, shown for the record in Figure S5c and superseded by the September re-run; the 1.3B-parameter and 354M training trajectories of Figure S4a,b, which remain because re-profiling them would require re-running the 50,000-step trainings and which are used only to say when the ratio first falls below 0.80; and the width × depth grid of Figure S10b, re-run in September 2026 with the causal mask and TF32 off. (3) *Canonical estimator, September 2026.* The hook-based token-local J^T^J power iteration of the Experimental procedures (survey_sigma1_v2, versions 2.2 and 2.3, which differ only in a validation mode for image models), in float32 with eager attention and TF32 disabled, on natural inputs at five inputs × eight positions with four restarts. Every other gain profile in the paper, the pretrained survey and census, the matched assay, the linearised response and the Pythia-410M checkpoint trajectory come from it. The May 2026 mechanism experiments of Figure S9 and Supplemental notes 1 to 4 report no gain profiles; they are reported from their retained result files, listed below, and cannot be re-measured because their checkpoints were not kept.

The linearised response was run twice on identical probes and directions: on 21 September 2026, recording ‖g‖^2^ and g^T^Fg, and on 25 September 2026, recording in addition mean(g), p^T^g and V so that the amplification–alignment split can be reported in the mean-centred logit gauge. The second run reproduced the first to a median relative deviation below 10^−6^ in g^T^Fg on the controlled checkpoints and to the stored digit against the pretrained assay on all 3,900 probes; Figure 3c and the centred values in the supplemental methods come from the second run, and the downstream factor from the first, the two agreeing within 2% on every model.

**Validation rerun.** The exact-decomposition validation behind Figure 1d,e was first run on 16 September 2026 (four transformers) and 18 September 2026 (ConvNeXt-tiny, ConvNeXt-base and Mamba-130M) in notebooks whose per-pair files were written to the instance disk and not kept; those runs survive as printed logs at three decimals. It was rerun on 25 September 2026 with the same script and settings (survey_sigma1_v2 v2.3, --validate; A100-40GB; 210 pairs in 27 minutes of compute) and every per-pair value written to persistent storage. The rerun reproduces the logged values pair by pair to a maximum relative difference of 1.9 × 10^−4^ (median 2–6 × 10^−5^ per model), which is the precision of the logs; its pooled maximum relative error is 3.0 × 10^−4^ where the logs gave 4.8 × 10^−4^, the difference being rounding, and every other statistic in the Experimental procedures is unchanged to the digit reported. Figure 1d,e and the Experimental procedures report the rerun.

The Mamba full-scan check of Figure S1 survived only as an image. It was rerun on 25 September 2026 with the original design (layers 0, 12 and 23; T = 4 and 8; five random-id inputs each) and one result file per pair. The estimator check reproduces (median relative error 2.5 × 10^−4^ against 2.1 × 10^−4^ in the original). The original right-hand panel compared the full-scan σ~1~ with an eight-probe finite-difference estimate of the one-step cross-position gain and reported 28–63×; the rerun reproduces that estimate and, reading the same Jacobian block exactly, gives 2.9–6.2× at layer 0, 1.9–4.3× at layer 12 and 18–30× at layer 23, so the original panel overstated the ratio by the factor by which eight random probes under-read a norm (Figure 1e). The regenerated figure shows the exact values, and the supplemental methods report the direction ratio on the plateau of the finite-difference curve rather than at the smallest scale, where float32 rounding dominates.

**Joint predictor baseline.** The joint baseline of Figure 2d,g and Table S3 (depth with the activation norm and the random-direction gain, with and without σ~1~) was added on 26 September 2026 by extending predictor_check.py and family_transfer.py with the two predictor sets; the values of the four original sets are unchanged, and the input-resampling intervals of the original sets in family_transfer.py are unchanged because the added sets draw their resamples from a separate generator.

**Consistency of the methods text with the tables.** Before this version was assembled, every number that appears both in the methods text and in a table was checked against the file that produces the table (Table S4 against the destroyed-data, switching, dependency-order, entropy-matched and 24-layer paragraphs; Table S3 against the regression, held-out-prediction and correlation paragraphs; Table S1 against the survey counts of the Results; Table 1 against the estimator and controlled-model methods). One discrepancy was found and corrected: the within-model rank correlations with block partialled out in Table S3c had been generated before the k-gram models were re-profiled and read 34 and 32 of 50 positive with medians 0.32 and 0.20; the regenerated table reads 35 and 35 of 50 with medians 0.32 and 0.19, which is what the methods text had stated. Two entries of Table 1 that had no stated source now have one: the identity-skip cost of 0.79 ± 0.02 of the branch-rotation cost is the mean ± s.d. over k ≥ 2 of the ratio of seed means in Table S4, and the shift of the middle-to-edge ratio by up to 0.31 when the probed token is isolated is Pythia-1.4B (0.70 to 1.01; Figure S2c).
