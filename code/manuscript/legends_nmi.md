## Table 1 | How each measurement choice changes the result

| Choice | Used here | Alternative | Effect of the alternative | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operator | power iteration on *J*^T^*J* (σ₁) | power iteration on *J* (spectral radius) | a median 2.1–3.6 times smaller per model, up to 18 times at a block; middle-to-edge ratio shifts by up to 0.40 | ED Fig. 2a,b |
| Search | power iteration | maximum over 64 random probes | σ₁ underestimated by 44–92% | Fig. 1e |
| Block or branch | block Jacobian *J* | branch Jacobian *J* − *I* | σ₁ changes by at most 0.52; ratio by at most 0.02 | Fig. 1e; ED Fig. 2d |
| Probe context | token-local, token in its natural context | isolated token, or whole-sequence Jacobian | isolated token shifts the ratio by up to 0.31; the whole-sequence Jacobian lowers it in 12 of 13 models, by up to 0.77 | ED Fig. 2c |
| Inputs | natural text, images or audio | random token ids or pixels | ratio changes by a median of 0.04 (largest 0.17) | ED Fig. 2c |
| Precision | float32 | float16; bfloat16 | float16 non-finite from some block onward in 7 of 10 decoders; bfloat16 inflates the ratio at small finite-difference steps (Pythia-160M 2.71 against 0.76) | ED Fig. 2e–h |
| Perturbation size | stated for each result | fixed fraction of ‖h‖ against per unit squared displacement | pooled slope −0.61 against 2.01 (controlled) and 1.89 against 2.12 (pretrained); within-model median ρ −0.02 against 0.60 and −0.11 against 0.56 | Fig. 2 |
| Intervention | branch rotation | identity skip; whole-block rotation | identity skip costs 0.79 ± 0.02 of branch rotation (k ≥ 2); whole-block rotation saturates at 8–10 nats in every decoder and cannot rank blocks | Methods |
